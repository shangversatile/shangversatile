## 🔬 Research Focus

I focus on **understanding and improving how AI systems reason**, rather than just improving accuracy.

### 🧠 Mechanistic Interpretability
- Understanding *how internal components of neural networks produce outputs*
- Moving beyond post-hoc explanations → **causal internal reasoning**

### 🔗 Causal Representation Learning
- Combining **Causal Inference + Deep Learning**
- Exploring structural reasoning inside models

### 🛡 Trustworthy AI
- Robustness under **distribution shift / noisy environments**
- Building **verifiable and evaluatable AI systems**
